Answers & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent terms designed for business travelers and vacationers.

What happens if my flight to Entebbe is delayed?

expand_more
We actively monitor all scheduled incoming flights via radar tracking. Your chauffeur automatically adjusts their arrival time according to your actual touchdown. You receive a complimentary 60-minute wait window starting once your aircraft safely lands.

Where will my chauffeur meet me at Entebbe Airport?

expand_more
Upon exiting baggage claim and customs into the main Arrivals terminal, your chauffeur will be waiting holding a high-visibility sign with your full name or company logo. We also send their photo and WhatsApp number before landing.

How and when do I pay for my transfer?

expand_more
Zero advance credit card payment is required. Payment is made smoothly upon arrival at your drop-off destination directly to the driver. We accept US Dollars (USD), Ugandan Shillings (UGX), Euros, and Visa/Mastercard.

Are highway expressway toll fees included in the price?

expand_more
Yes, 100%. All quoted fares include the Entebbe-Kampala Expressway electronic toll card fees, fuel, driver fees, and airport terminal parking tickets. There are zero surprise out-of-pocket expenses.
